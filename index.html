Youqi Liao (Martin Y.Liao)  

I will begin my ph.D. at the State Key Laboratory of information Engineering in Surveying, Mapping and Remote Sensing (LIESMARS), Wuhan University. Before that, I received my B.S. degree from Wuhan University in 2023. My research interests include place recognition, geo-localization and spatial reasoning.
